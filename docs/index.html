CoreKit

For Android and Windows · by Junaid Shaheen

CoreKit is one toolkit for looking after your devices:

  • Health Inspector – battery, storage, memory, processor and sensors, with a health score.
  • Internet Troubleshooter – find and fix Wi-Fi, DNS and HTTPS problems, plus a speed test.
  • Safe Browser – private profiles and your own proxy or VPN server, with leak checks.
  • Activity Log – see which apps were opened and when your device was unlocked while you were away.
  • Remote Access – view and control your own phone or PC on the same Wi-Fi with a one-time code, with calls between them.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms & Conditions.
